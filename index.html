Forgiven to Live

Forgiveness, Repentance, and the Recovery of Moral Life

In a world where wrongdoing is excused, truth softened and responsibility avoided, the language of sin, repentance and forgiveness has become unfamiliar. Yet beneath the noise of modern life lies a deeper reality: fractured relationships, wounded consciences and broken communities.

Forgiven to Live draws on Scripture, theology, ethical reflection and lived realities to show how forgiveness opens the way to renewal, for individuals, families, churches and nations alike.

About

About the author

Ngoni M. Mupfurutsa is a Zimbabwean minister, theologian and author based in Harare. He holds a BA (Hons) in Religious Studies and Theology, with research on schism and renewal in African Initiated Churches, and is completing postgraduate studies at Africa University.

His writing aims to be intellectually rigorous and spiritually nourishing at the same time: serious enough for the academy, clear enough for the congregation, and always in service of the Church's life and witness in Africa and beyond.
